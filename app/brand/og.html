Best answer · Evidence
A pedestrian sued a delivery company after one of its vans struck her in a crosswalk. The company offers the driver's supervisor, who rode along forty times over six years and saw the driver stop at that sign every time. The pedestrian objects.
Is the testimony admissible?
ANo, because the supervisor did not witness the collision.
BYes, because evidence of a person's habit is admissible to prove conduct in conformity with it.
CYes, because a civil defendant may offer its employee's good character.
DNo, because prior conduct is never admissible to prove conduct on an occasion.
